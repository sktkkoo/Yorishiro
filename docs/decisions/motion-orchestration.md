# Recorded motion orchestration

Status: implementation extended on `fix/motion-feedback`; comparative human evaluation pending. The [recurring quality review](motion-quality-review.md) separates measured defects, acting choices and unobserved acceptance criteria.

The [quality strategy](motion-quality-strategy.md) sets the current priority: restore faithful full-body/finger replay from the original recordings, then review Idle, listening, conversation and their handoffs. Animates' music-synchronized dancing is a quality reference, not the first required feature. Current runtime improvements are foundations, not proof that the material or product meets that reference.

Yorishiro now uses recorded VRMA motion as the main idle and conversational body performance. Procedural breathing, gaze and small offsets supplement the recording. Previously those offsets overwrote the spine and arm rotations even when a recorded clip had most of the weight; that mixing error is fixed.

## Runtime

`Body` warms the recordings admitted by its character motion profile from the local asset store, plus an independent standing foundation and the optional reviewed body bundle. Catalog membership alone does not grant playback. The bundled automatic upper-body profile is bound to the reviewed Yori model; other avatars retain calibrated standing support without receiving these unreviewed upper performances. `MotionDirector` queries an inspectable six-dimensional semantic index (calmness, attention, reflection, affiliation, emphasis, movement energy), filters context and cooldown, and samples at most five eligible candidates. This is a small local vector search, not a language-model embedding or an external vector database. No paid API, local model download or extra inference is needed. Existing assistant transcript interpretation supplies speech intent; optional inline text tags are unnecessary. The shared `VoicePlayer.say(text)` path also passes text through the same local resolver, so MCP speech, Quick Chat and persona speech using the existing OS `SayTtsEngine` can drive motion without a realtime API. Cues remain pending during synthesis and acquire ownership only after Web Audio starts. Stop, cancellation and playback-owner changes release them; the unclocked native fallback does not pretend to provide gesture timing.

The reviewed Yori body base alternates two compatible standing units. Quiet idle suppresses their large head and torso excursions while retaining the source pelvis and legs. Normal uses quiet torso/head gains of 0.09/0.03; Lively preserves the former Normal values of 0.18/0.06. Over retains 0.65/0.45 with accents up to the original 1/1. See the [intensity calibration](terminal-idle-intensity-calibration.md) for the public-setting mapping and preserved speech weights. A separate posture schedule considers HandOnHip after 15–25 seconds of eligible idle, listening or thinking time, for 8–12 seconds, with a 180-second reuse cooldown. It has no preferred first pick or unrestricted attentive fallback. Upper Idle is excluded because its repeated symmetric shoulder lifts remain after static stance calibration; its independently calibrated lower support remains available. Survey and Warrior Stretch share a rarer 90–150-second schedule. The [idle dynamics](recorded-idle-dynamics.md) and [Mixamo usage review](mixamo-motion-usage.md) describe their separate timing and admission rules. Immediate repetition is excluded for ordinary idle/background selection; reviewed speech one-shots may repeat after their own cooldown.

Thinking, speaking, interruption, ownership and reduced motion each have explicit behavior. A voice utterance owns its gesture; a nearby facial cue does not restart or truncate it when gesture selection is temporarily suppressed. After a finite speech gesture naturally finishes during continued audible speech, the director immediately reconsiders the conversational background while the player's recovery fade continues. Physical compatibility, availability and history still govern admission. Idle and manual-owner handoffs retain their settling period. Leaving audible speech releases the conversational background even if every attentive replacement is missing, cooling down or physically incompatible.

Neutral speech also has a recorded performance. Actual audio start selects an explanatory background from Chatting and Chatting 2, with 10–18 second dwell times and weight 0.85 before the user's intensity setting. This does not require an emotional keyword. The earlier approximately 0.4 weights visibly weakened authored hand poses; [target-avatar gain review](conversation-performance-gain-review.md) records the comparison and its limits. Chatting 2 is classified as animated conversation, with higher energy and emphasis, rather than quiet reflection. Facial-cue expiry releases only the face; finite body gestures use their authored end, or a low-velocity exit 6–6.5 seconds after entry for longer recordings, followed by their fade. The four reviewed Mixamo one-shots may finish their authored recovery after natural audio completion. New speech, interruption, explicit stop, disposal and playback-owner loss release the appropriate old gesture without cancelling a newer owner. Grounded speaking also remains eligible during simultaneous reading/writing/running tool activity, while explicit motion owners and claims keep priority. Once conversation phases are supplied, they take precedence over a lip-sync analyser that stays active for an entire realtime connection; listening and silent idle therefore resume while the connection remains open.

`AnimationPlayer` retargets and analyzes quaternion poses and angular velocities once per clip variant, with bounded sampling. Before the weighted draw, automatic selection compares eligible candidates with the actual mixed upper-body pose and signed angular velocity at the requested contribution. Incompatible or unprepared candidates are excluded; transition cost adjusts the remaining sampling weights before the final top five. The chosen incoming phase is passed to playback. Authored finite gestures retain their beginning. Explicit matched playback without a selected entry can also search a short outgoing low-speed window. Incoming and outgoing weights follow smoothstep ramps without changing playback speed to fit unrelated clip lengths. Asynchronous loading and delayed entry remain conditional on current ownership. Old fades are retired when an external animation owner takes over.

Looping upper-body and fallback-foundation recordings receive a cached tail correction. The final 0.4–0.8 seconds converge to the first pose and its angular velocity using a quintic envelope. Original non-looping performances stay intact; the reviewed finite body units use contact-gated handoffs instead. The loop correction closes the internal quaternion seam. It is a bounded alteration of the end of a looping recording, not generated motion or an IK solver.

Unambiguous adjacent body units from the same source, with identical context,
axial reference, hand policy and contact annotations, play as one uninterrupted
source range. Their authored unit labels still advance from the actual phase.
This avoids an asynchronous endpoint hold and a fade from a frozen pose at an
internal split. A different source, gap, policy or ambiguous continuation remains
a separate handoff. The extended endpoint must pass the existing target contact
gate; if it fails, the original shorter unit retains its independent eligibility.
The discontinuous source wrap still uses the validated transition.

## Contact and scope

On the exact reviewed Yori model, the optional body bundle supplies two finite units from Looking Around at 7.0833–15.6667 and 15.6667–23.3667 seconds. Each unit splits the same source clip into synchronized upper and lower actions. Source hips XYZ, leg motion and timing remain intact; automatic upper-body performances duck the base's upper contribution while its legs continue. Incoming units receive a constant XZ placement offset and must pass target foot/toe position, height, support-speed and sampled fade checks. Failed admission retains the supported outgoing pose. The source stance is established before initial display, and the bundle is pinned to the exact animation and model hashes. See [recorded body playback](recorded-body-playback.md) for ownership, bootstrap and native activity behavior.

The current bundle has only two compatible standing units, with a roughly 16.3-second lower-body cycle. During conversation, its Looking Around legs continue beneath Chatting upper-body acting; Chatting's different source stance is not admitted as a body-base replacement. Missing bundles use the separate calibrated lower-body `Idle` standing foundation when available. Automatic upper performances additionally require an admitted character profile; the bundled Yori profile does not authorize them on other avatars. Explicit full-body performances and animation claims take precedence over either supporting path. Whole-character breathing displacement is attenuated by recorded weight on either layer.

The fallback standing foundation first transfers Idle's small recorded rotation changes relative to its first frame onto the target's normalized rest pose. This avoids a 10–16 cm foot displacement caused by blending directly into the source's different initial stance. After loop conditioning, target-skeleton forward kinematics recovers the shared displacement of both ankles and toes and adds the inverse to the hips position. It does not use the legacy Idle conversion's zero reference hip height. This preparation runs once on private transform copies, without posing the live avatar. Static upper Idle calibration removes its fixed shoulder bias but does not remove its two symmetric shoulder-lift cycles; this is why it is now excluded from automatic upper-body admission. Intentional asymmetry in other performances is preserved.

These restrictions are deliberate: the [29-asset audit](motion-assets-analysis.json) found that removing hip translation from several existing recordings changes foot motion substantially. Low-speed frames can still differ in foot placement by tens of centimetres. Foundation calibration and grounding apply only to the reviewed quiet Idle, whose lower-body changes stay within about 1.12 degrees. The separate [offline target adaptation](recorded-target-contact-adaptation.md) and runtime contact gate qualify specific standing units; they do not provide arbitrary locomotion, dance joins or floor collision. Handoffs from an arbitrary explicit leg stance can still move the feet or prevent the recorded base from resuming. Moving-base semantic scoring also omits the unreliable static-reference velocity term; full velocity-aware layered prediction remains future work.

The default profile admits six speech recordings: Thankful, Chatting, Chatting 2, Fist Pump, Thoughtful Head Shake and Hands Forward Gesture. The three admitted Mixamo speech gestures play once from their authored beginning. Shrugging is excluded from automatic playback following user review. HandOnHip is posture-only; rare Survey and the five-second Warrior Stretch are separately admitted occasional programs. Idle remains a source for lower support, not an automatically selected upper performance. Idle Conversation, Watching and whole Looking Around upper performances are excluded after source/target acting review. Sad, Texting and the full Warrior Idle remain manual-only; Sad cannot safely return to the current supporting stance, and Texting requires a phone prop. Selection, phase matching and loop correction remain separate operations. The [candidate compatibility audit](motion-candidate-compatibility-metrics.json) records an earlier catalog's upper-body rotation/velocity checks; it does not measure full-body contacts or certify the final catalog. The [Mixamo review](mixamo-motion-review.json) records the earlier source audit; current automatic admission is in `motion-profile.ts` and may be stricter than that audit. Source assets remain in `../Yorishiro-assets`, following existing distribution and licensing rules.

The [standing measurements](standing-idle-grounding-metrics.json) replay the real mixer at 120 Hz on Yori, including SDK-updated raw bones. Across two loops the calibrated, grounded foundation's maximum foot drift is 0 / 0.259 / 0.645 / 1.286 mm at weights 0 / 0.2 / 0.5 / 1. The 1.2-second start fade stays within 1.286 mm, compared with 161.302 mm for an uncalibrated absolute-pose fade; fading out and reducing gain from 1 to 0.2 stay within 0.299 / 0.328 mm respectively. These tests use phase-zero starts and no procedural layers; they establish the reviewed foundation's behavior, not universal foot locking or human preference.

## Use and verification

Run from the worktree:

```sh
npm run motion:lab
npm run motion:audit
npm run motion:seams
npm run motion:grounding
```

The lab is at `http://127.0.0.1:1437/motion-lab.html`. It compares the same Yori avatar, camera and lighting with the library disabled/enabled and lets a reviewer exercise semantic gestures. It is a development entry point, not part of the release bundle. “Export observations” saves selected clips, timing and sampled head/hand positions. `scripts/capture-motion-lab.mjs` can automate screenshots and, with `--film`, a 30-second comparison video using a separately available Playwright/browser and ffmpeg. `YORISHIRO_PLAYWRIGHT_MODULE`, `YORISHIRO_CHROME_PATH`, `YORISHIRO_MOTION_LAB_URL` and `YORISHIRO_MOTION_QA_DIR` configure those local tools and outputs. The default output is the Git-ignored `.motion-review/` directory.

For audible review, run `node scripts/prepare-motion-speech-sample.mjs` once on macOS, then click “Play local speech sample”. The generator requires the locally installed Japanese Kyoko voice and writes a 24.052-second WAV plus text manifest under `.motion-review/speech-sample/` without playing sound or downloading a voice. Both lanes receive the same VoicePlayer audio, mouth sample and expression cues through the existing runtime bridge. No microphone or autoplay is used. Cues begin on the actual playback clock; phrase offsets remain estimates from the existing resolver, not word alignment. Stop, interruption, pausing and changing playback mode release the sample. A browser frame gap above 250 ms also stops it instead of replaying delayed gestures against advanced audio.

`motionIntensity` scales automatic upper-body strength and quiet axial dynamics. Positive values, including 0.95, retain full supporting leg weight and source speed. Zero pauses the reviewed body base in place, suppresses its upper contribution and stops selecting new units; a positive value resumes the same supported phase. Normal (1), Lively (2) and Over (3) have different axial amplitudes and accent frequency. `Body.setMotionLibraryEnabled(false)` allows a controlled baseline or an internal opt-out without cancelling a persona/MCP-owned performance. Advanced callers can set `MotionOptions.mask`, `transition` and `maxTransitionDelayMs` through the existing motion slot API. Semantic requests are currently an internal Body API.

Reviewed full-body one-shots can explicitly request `rootMotion: "preserve"` with immediate entry and looping disabled. The default remains in-place. This opt-in retains a validated finite hips XYZ track with the source-to-target rest-height scale; invalid source geometry or unsupported masked/matched/looping requests fail rather than silently losing translation. It does not automatically admit a clip to the body bundle or semantic catalog. Source fidelity, target adaptation and acting quality remain separate review gates.

Tests cover retrieval/diversity, real-mixer fade envelopes, phase selection, loop position/velocity continuity, ownership races, same-clip replay, claims, missing assets, conversational boundaries and reduced motion. The asset audit additionally checks its FK against the official retargeted Three.js mixer. The [loop metrics](motion-loop-seam-metrics.json) quantify improvement over the original source clip at its boundary; they are not measurements of Animates or human preference.

## Acceptance

Implementation and regression checks are distinct from the requested acceptance criterion. A comparison against installed Animates is still required to establish a clear advantage in naturalness and presence. Follow the [evaluation protocol and observed Animates conditions](motion-orchestration-evaluation.md). More motion variety or a lower boundary discontinuity alone does not establish that advantage.

## Follow-up conversational handoff fixes, 2026-09-14

An independent review found two interruptions of recorded conversation that the earlier aggregate checks did not expose. First, immediate Web Audio replacement ended the old utterance before announcing the new source's successful start. The bridge consequently emitted speaking → responding → speaking within one JavaScript task, and Body released the background performance. VoicePlayer now announces successful playout before retiring the previous operation. Old utterance cues still release, while continuous speech keeps its background. Failed starts and replacement by a non-speech clip still retire the old owner; no grace timer was added. Actual muted Chrome playback reproduced the unwanted phase at audio time 1.216 s before the fix and preserved speaking throughout the handoff afterward. Disposal still returned to idle. The real VoicePlayer/bridge/Body regression also covers natural completion, pending synthesis and start failure.

Second, an active finite gesture extended the ambient deadline by 2.5 s on every blocked frame. Its natural completion left that idle-settling deadline behind even while speech continued. Body now asks for reconsideration only after natural completion of a speech-expression motion, during grounded assistant speech, with no remaining motion owner. It does not shorten the authored end, bounded quiet exit or recovery fade, and does not override a rejected physical transition. Tests retain the original behavior for idle/listening, cancellation and manual preemption.

In separate 24.052-second Kyoko runs through the real lab, the pre-fix finite Conversation was followed by sampled inactive upper-body slots from 15.883–18.349 s; Chatting 2 resumed at 18.449 s. After the fix, Conversation transitioned to Chatting at the next approximately 100 ms observation, 15.916 s, without a sampled inactive interval until audio ended. Different real frame timing can change weighted selections, so these runs prove the scheduling defect and its removal, not a matched-trajectory perceptual preference. Artifacts are `.motion-review/speech-handoff/{before,after}.json` and `resume-{before,after}/`; both browser checks reported no page errors.

The combined affected suites passed 136 tests in six files, with TypeScript and Biome checks. Logs are `.motion-review/speech-handoff-{combined-vitest,tsc,biome}.log`. The earlier 3,047-test production-build record applies to `22720746`; it is not a claim that the full suite or production build was rerun after these two scoped fixes. The old v3 film also predates them. Animates conversation comparison remains unexecuted: a read-only recheck still found Accessibility untrusted, and CUA initialization remained unavailable. No permission settings or app inputs were changed.
