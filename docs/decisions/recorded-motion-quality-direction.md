# Recorded motion quality direction: preserve source performance first

Research checked on 2026-09-14. This is a proposed content and evaluation direction, not a claim that the existing implementation outperforms Animates. The user cites Animates' conversational gestures and music-responsive dance as examples of the expected quality. The first implementation priority remains idle, listening, and conversation with natural full-body timing. Music and dance set a broader quality benchmark; they are not the first feature expansion. Quiet-idle contact improvements alone do not satisfy that goal.

## Production direction

First recover and compare the original FBX recordings already credited to Rokoko and Mixamo with their converted VRMA output. Yorishiro already has professional captured full-body/finger material; the immediate question is how much of that performance the converter and runtime preserve. Check source rest skeleton, units, root/hips translation, coordinate conversion, finger tracks, timebase, and foot support on the same source recording before acquiring replacements. Then select a small coherent library, adapt the performances to Yori, annotate usable phrases, and coordinate them with listening and speech. Local procedural motion should provide contact correction, gaze, breathing, and transition assistance. It should not replace the underlying performance or fabricate a large motion vocabulary from one poor recording.

The single-video pose-estimation plus Blender-cleanup prototype is not the proposed production source. Its uncertainty, occlusion, missing depth/contact information, and hand reconstruction limitations cannot be erased by smoothing. The existing source manifest already identifies those Pexels fixtures as diagnostics, not production motion. Keep them available for regression research without treating them as acceptable content.

Before increasing library size or introducing a learned generator, prove three short target-avatar performances: a sustained idle, an attentive listening response, and a spoken explanation with meaningful hand shapes, body weight changes, and emphasis. Review each at normal playback speed from full-body and hand-visible views. A clip must first be compelling on Yori itself; successful conversion, low joint jitter, or a populated vector index cannot establish that.

## Research datasets are a different category

[Bandai Namco Research's dataset](https://github.com/BandaiNamcoResearchInc/Bandai-Namco-Research-Motiondataset) documents capture with professional actors and studio post-processing, but the data are CC BY-NC 4.0. Its MIT Blender visualization code does not change those data rights. Treat it as a research reference, not a commercially licensed content library.

[BEAT](https://github.com/PantoMatrix/BEAT) provides a useful research specification: synchronized motion, face, audio, text, emotion, and semantic annotations, including full-body/hand data. A publicly accessible repository and impressive benchmark are not sufficient permission to embed its data, trained weights, avatar assets, or SMPL-X/FLAME dependencies in a product. Those rights were not established here; no production adoption is proposed.

## Existing local assets: what was actually verified

The sibling `Yorishiro-assets/animations` contains 29 VRMA files. Every file declares 30 finger rotation channels. A read-only official-loader audit finds all 30 fingers change by more than one degree in `Idle Conversation`, `Idle Chatting`, `Idle Arguing`, and `Idle Listening To Music`; their largest finger changes are approximately 34.63, 45.68, 60.59 and 44.28 degrees. `Leaning` has static finger channels. Channel existence and angular range do not establish anatomically convincing hand animation, but the library should not be described as universally lacking finger motion.

Twenty-one of the 29 clips have zero source rest-hips height, making the standard source-to-target root-translation ratio undefined. That conversion issue is separate from source-performance quality. The runtime's general translation stripping can discard real weight shifts; the reviewed quiet-Idle correction is not a substitute for repairing general full-body import.

The authoritative repository record is [CREDITS.md](../../CREDITS.md): ten named everyday clips come from Rokoko, nine from Adobe Mixamo, and seven from VRoid Project. The Rokoko FBX-to-VRMA conversion via VRMAConverter is explicitly recorded. All 29 inspected GLB asset headers lack embedded copyright/license metadata, but that does **not** mean the project lacks provenance. Preserve CREDITS and add original-file hashes/conversion settings when the source FBX is recovered.

CREDITS identifies the seven `VRMA_0*` assets as the pack that [VRoid officially announced](https://vroid.com/news/6HozzBIV0KkcKf9dc1fZGW), with the required project attribution and distribution restrictions recorded there. The three `Conversational Emphasis` variants are authoring/prototype outputs, not evidence of a new high-quality production performance. The local `mocap-prototype/sources/manifest.json` expressly labels its video fixtures as diagnostic or non-production.

## Proposed acceptance gates

1. **Source performance:** inspect the actual clip at normal speed, with hands and feet visible. Reject unconvincing posture, repetitive mechanical hand articulation, weak gesture intention, obvious capture noise, and poor dance weight transfers before retargeting work.
2. **Retargeted performance:** compare source and Yori from front and side; check wrist/palm orientation, thumb opposition, finger collisions, elbow/shoulder deformation, foot penetration and support. Preserve root motion for dance and genuine stance changes. Standing-idle rest calibration is not a general dance retargeting method.
3. **Phrase metadata:** record stance/contact, entry/exit pose and velocity, hand shape, semantic function, stroke/hold/recovery intervals, amplitude/style, tempo, beat phase and musical bar boundaries. Avoid treating sentence-level tags or top-five random retrieval alone as an execution schedule.
4. **Speech behavior:** align an emphasized gesture's stroke with the relevant spoken phrase, allow preparation before the stroke and recovery after it, and use listening responses when the other party speaks. Test interruptions and consecutive utterances. Random motion variety cannot compensate for mistimed meaning.
5. **Future music benchmark, after idle/listening/conversation:** retain authored choreographic phrases, select by compatible tempo/style, and transition at musically and physically compatible boundaries. Check slow, medium and fast tracks, starts/stops, and tempo changes. Small retiming should preserve weight transfer; extreme stretch should choose another phrase.
6. **Comparative judgement:** first run the same idle, listening and conversation scenarios against Animates, document capture conditions, and collect separate ratings for motion naturalness, timing/meaning, hands, physical support, variety and presence. Require convincing results in each prioritized scenario; do not average weak conversation performance away with improved idle metrics. Music/dance can later test whether the architecture preserves more demanding full-body performances.

These are proposed quality gates for the next content pass. The observed sub-millimeter/millimeter standing-idle improvements are useful engineering evidence, while the user's target of clearly superior overall motion remains unverified.
