# Candidate compatibility before sampling

Status: local rotation continuity improvement, 2026-09-14. This does not establish authored gesture timing, full-body contact quality, or superiority to Animates. The primary direction remains [motion quality strategy](motion-quality-strategy.md).

The director previously selected a semantically suitable recording first. The player then searched that recording for a mechanically similar phase. A better candidate could lose the draw before its actual pose was considered; during a crossfade the latest action alone also differs from the visible mixed pose.

`AnimationPlayer.evaluateTransition(ref, options)` now returns a cached `{ cost, startTimeSec }` or `null`. Body supplies this synchronous callback to the director. Candidate evaluation occurs before the final weighted draw, with no asset loading, profile preparation, or mutation of avatar bones. Cold variants and incomplete skeleton matches are unavailable; when no candidate is compatible the current performance remains and selection history is not consumed.

After each mixer update, reusable buffers record the actual local bone rotations before procedural overlays. Two adjacent samples provide angular velocity in the bone parent's axes; a pause, zero delta, or an interval outside 1/240–1/10 second suppresses the unreliable velocity term. This includes the contribution of overlapping actions. Candidate rotations are blended against the same restoration pose that the mixer captured, using the requested weight. Candidate velocity is measured from these weighted rotations and uses the requested playback speed.

The cost is the joint-weighted mean squared quaternion angle plus `0.08` times mean squared angular-velocity difference. Fingers have weight `0.08` each; major body joints have weight `1`. The director applies `exp(-4 * cost)` to the existing semantic/history sampling weight. The player rejects an entry when pose RMS exceeds `0.65 rad`, any major joint exceeds `1.2 rad`, velocity RMS exceeds `2.5 rad/s`, or total cost exceeds `0.75`. These are conservative engineering guards against large local discontinuities. They are not validated thresholds for perceived naturalness.

Matched background recordings search at most 48 cached mechanical entry candidates and choose the least costly entry that passes every guard. Immediate one-shots only evaluate the beginning. Existing low-velocity samples are not semantic annotations: a gesture's held peak can be almost motionless. This change does not create approved preparation, stroke, recovery, contact, or exit windows. Such annotations must be reviewed against the actual performance and bound to the source/prepared asset hashes before authored motion-unit scheduling can replace the current duration guard.

## Reproduction and measured range

Run `node scripts/measure-motion-candidate-compatibility.mjs`. It reads the installed Yori model and current catalog VRMAs, reconstructs the target humanoid without a renderer, applies the application's resting pose, and uses the real Three mixer. The compact [metrics](motion-candidate-compatibility-metrics.json) include model/asset hashes; all individual measurements remain in ignored `.motion-review/motion-candidate-compatibility-full.json`. Input assets are unchanged.

The current three Rokoko conversational recordings use `0.85` weight. Across 949 measured requests, 720 have a compatible entry: 11/13 from rest, 306/468 from steady recordings, and 403/468 during an actual half-completed 1.2-second crossfade. Unconstrained minimum cost has median `0.1223`, 95th percentile `0.4134`, and maximum `0.7382`; the maximum individual main-joint mismatch is `2.253 rad`. At least one neutral-speech candidate remains in 71/73 sampled source states. The two exclusions are the deliberate HandOnHip performance at its 10% and 25% phases. This audit does not simulate cooldown/history exclusions and cannot guarantee a candidate at every runtime moment.

Regression tests use the real mixer to check a non-identity captured rest pose at partial weight, overlapping fades, and immunity to later procedural offsets. Pure analysis tests distinguish opposite rotation axes, compare velocities in a common parent frame, find an alternative admissible phase, and preserve the entry-zero policy for immediate gestures. Cold variants fail closed without invoking the loader.

Full-body source fidelity and target replay remain separate gates. The [source-faithful Conversation measurements](source-faithful-conversation-metrics.json) preserve the authored root/body relationship, but their technical 0.1-second prefix trim is not an approved semantic in-point. This upper-body evaluator does not authorize that new full-body recording for production or infer support compatibility from local quaternion similarity.
